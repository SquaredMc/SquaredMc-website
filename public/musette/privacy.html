Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026  ·  Applies to the Musette iPhone app and these web pages.

The short version

  • There's no account and no Musette server. Your Apple Health data, your goal and your daily picks stay on your iPhone, apart from anything you add to your calendar, and we never receive any of it.
  • For the weather forecast, Musette sends Apple an approximate location, rounded to about a kilometre. Nothing is sent to us.
  • If you connect your calendar, Musette reads only when you're busy. When you add sessions, it puts them, each with its reason, in a Training calendar that syncs with your calendar account like your other calendars.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Musette is an iPhone app that suggests which Zwift workout to ride today, with the reason, based on your training and recovery in Apple Health and the event you're training for. It's built by SQUAREDMC LTD ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales with company number 16175464, whose registered office is at 17 Poole Road, Epsom, KT19 9SQ.

This policy explains what information the Musette app and these web pages use, why, and your rights. Most of it never leaves your iPhone, so we don't hold it. For the little we do hold, described in sections 10 and 13, we're the controller. If you have any questions, email us at hello@squaredmc.com.

2. Apple Health

If you allow it, Musette reads these from Apple Health: workouts, heart rate, cycling power, cycling distance, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, and your date of birth.

  • What it's for. Musette uses them to work out your recent training load, how recovered you look, and which workout fits today. Your date of birth gives your age, which is used only to estimate your maximum heart rate if you haven't entered it.
  • Read only. Musette never writes to Apple Health.
  • In the background. Musette also refreshes in the background, when iOS tells it new workouts, sleep or heart data have arrived and early each morning, so today's pick, the widget and the forecast are up to date when you look.
  • On your iPhone. Your Health data is used on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us, and it isn't used for advertising. The one way something based on it can leave your iPhone is the reason for a session you've added to your calendar, described in section 5.

You can change what Musette can read at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy, then Musette.

3. Information you enter

Your goal (an event's name, date and type, or "just keep me fit"), the time you have to train on each day of the week, and any rider numbers you enter (FTP, maximum heart rate and threshold heart rate) are stored only on your iPhone. They aren't sent to us.

4. Location and weather

The Week tab can mark which days suit riding outside, using a weather forecast. For that it needs a location, which you choose to give.

  • Approximate and occasional. The first time you open the Week tab, Musette asks for location access "While Using the App". If you allow it, Musette takes a single reading and rounds it to two decimal places, about a kilometre, and keeps that rounded position as the place for your forecast. You can take a new reading later with Use my approximate location. Musette doesn't follow where you go, and it never asks for "Always" access.
  • Or a town. You can type a town instead. The text you type goes to Apple to find the place.
  • What Apple receives. To fetch the forecast, Musette sends the rounded position to Apple Weather every few hours, including when it refreshes in the background. To show the town's name, it may also send the rounded position to Apple's place lookup service. It downloads Apple Weather's logo to show with the forecast, without your location. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.

You can remove the location at any time on the Week tab: tap Change location, then Forget the forecast location. You can turn off location access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Musette. Without a location the week loses its indoor and outdoor labels, and everything else keeps working. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

5. Calendar

Connecting your calendar is optional, and Musette works fully without it. If you connect it, iOS asks you to give Musette full access to your calendars.

  • What Musette reads. Only the start and end times of events over the coming days, to find when you're busy. It doesn't read event titles, notes or locations. For invitations, it checks only whether you've declined, so declined ones don't count. Events marked free and all-day events are ignored too.
  • What Musette writes. When you tap Add to calendar, Musette creates a calendar called "Training" and adds the training sessions for the coming days to it: the time, a short title such as "Indoor ride · 60 min" or, on the day, the workout's name, and a note with the reason for the session. The reason can mention your event and how recovered you look, such as short sleep or a low heart rate variability, so it goes wherever your calendar syncs. Once you've added sessions, Musette keeps today's event up to date with the day's pick, including when it refreshes in the background. It updates or removes only the events it added, and never changes your other events.
  • Where that calendar lives. Musette creates the Training calendar in your default calendar account, or, if it can't, in another calendar account or on your iPhone only. If it's in iCloud, Google or another online account, the Training calendar and its events sync to it like your other calendars, and that provider's privacy policy applies to them. No calendar data is sent to us.

Deleting Musette doesn't delete the Training calendar. You can delete it in the Calendar app. You can turn off calendar access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Musette.

6. Notifications

If you turn on Remind me Sundays at 18:00 on the Week tab, Musette schedules one notification a week on your iPhone, saying your week is ready. It doesn't send any other notifications, and they don't come from a server. You can turn it off on the Week tab or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Musette.

7. The widget

The app and its Home Screen widget share a storage area on your iPhone that only they can use. It holds the information listed in section 8, and the widget shows today's pick from it.

8. What's stored on your iPhone

Musette keeps these on your iPhone so it can work without an account:

  • your goal, your week's training times and your rider numbers;
  • today's pick and the last 30 days of picks;
  • the coming days' training slots, and which calendar events Musette added;
  • the rounded location or town for the forecast, and the latest forecast;
  • a technical log of what the app did, such as when it refreshed, including some of the numbers it worked from, such as your heart rate numbers, FTP and event date; and
  • if the diagnostics screen in Settings is opened, a file listing your recent workouts' times, durations, distances, power and heart rate, used to check the app's calculations.

None of this is sent to us. Like other apps' data, it's included in your iPhone's backups to iCloud or a computer if you make them. Deleting Musette deletes all of it, apart from the Training calendar described in section 5.

9. Zwift

Musette suggests workouts from Zwift's library. Its Zwift button opens the Zwift app, or Zwift Companion if that's what you have, or Zwift's page on the App Store if neither is installed. Nothing is sent to Zwift, and Musette doesn't connect to your Zwift account. Zwift's own privacy policy applies when you use Zwift. Musette isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Zwift, Inc.

10. The beta

While Musette is in beta, we invite testers ourselves through Apple's TestFlight. If you're one of them:

  • we hold the name and email address we used to invite you, in our Apple developer account;
  • TestFlight tells us when you install the beta and how often it's used, and shares crash reports with us, along with your device model and iOS version; and
  • if you send feedback through TestFlight, we receive what you write and any screenshot you attach.

We use this only to run the beta and improve Musette. We remove you from TestFlight when the beta ends or if you ask us to. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy also apply.

11. Usage, analytics and crash data

Musette doesn't include any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Apart from the beta, described in section 10, the only crash reports we see come from Apple: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, which don't identify you. If we ever add analytics, we'll update this policy before that collection starts.

12. What we don't collect

There's no account, so we don't collect your name, email address or phone number (unless you're a beta tester or you email us). Musette doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It doesn't show ads, track you across other companies' apps and websites, or sell or share your information.

If you email us, we'll have your email address and whatever you include, and we'll use them only to reply to you.

14. Legal basis and your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we rely on our legitimate interests to hold beta testers' details and TestFlight reports so we can run the beta, and to reply when you email us. Your Health data, location and calendar never reach us, so we don't process them.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. For beta testers we can give you, correct or delete what we hold. Everything else lives only on your iPhone, where you control it, and deleting Musette removes it.

To make a request, email hello@squaredmc.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

15. Children

Musette isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.
