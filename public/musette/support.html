Quick fixes

No pick, or the pick doesn't reflect my riding

  • Musette needs to read Apple Health. In the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Musette, and turn everything on.
  • Your rides need to reach Apple Health. They do from an Apple Watch, the Zwift app on the same iPhone, Garmin Connect or Wahoo with Health sync on, or Strava with its permission to write to Health on.
  • Set your goal on the Goal tab, and your numbers under Settings → Rider. Enter your FTP so rides with power count properly. Musette estimates your heart rate numbers if you leave them empty.
  • Picks get better as Apple Health holds more of your riding. Musette's fitness trend looks back 42 days.

Open Zwift doesn't open the workout

Open Zwift opens the Zwift app, not a particular workout. Musette shows where to find the workout in Zwift's workout menu. If you only have Zwift Companion, the button opens that instead. If neither is installed, it says Get Zwift and opens Zwift's page on the App Store.

The Week tab has no indoor or outdoor days

The forecast needs a location. Musette asks for one the first time you open the Week tab. If you said no, type a town on the Week tab instead, or turn location on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Musette and tap Use my approximate location. Musette only ever uses an approximate location.

My calendar

  • Tap Add to calendar on the Week tab. Musette adds the sessions to a calendar called "Training", and from then on plans around the busy times in your other calendars.
  • To stop planning around your calendar, tap Stop in Musette's Settings tab.
  • Deleting Musette doesn't delete the Training calendar. To remove it, open the Calendar app, tap Calendars, tap the info button next to Training, and choose Delete Calendar.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit or the add button, then Add Widget, and choose Musette. "Today's pick" comes in small and medium sizes.

The Sunday reminder

Turn on Remind me Sundays at 18:00 on the Week tab for one notification a week when your week is ready. Turn it off in the same place.

Deleting your data

Everything Musette stores is on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it, apart from the Training calendar, which you can delete as described above. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Your rides in Apple Health.
  • Zwift, with its own account and subscription, to ride the workouts indoors.
