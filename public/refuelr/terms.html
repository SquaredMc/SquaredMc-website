2. What Refuelr does

Refuelr shows fuel prices for petrol stations near you, or near a place you search for, and highlights the cheapest. It also estimates what you could save on a tank compared with the average price in your search area. That saving is an estimate, based on the prices we hold and the tank size you choose.

3. Price information

Prices and station details come from the UK Government's Fuel Finder scheme, where fuel retailers report their own prices. We refresh them every few hours.

  • Prices can change at any time, so a price may be out of date, or wrong, by the time you see it or arrive.
  • The price on the pump is the price you pay. Always check it before you fill up.
  • Refuelr doesn't hold opening hours or stock levels. A station may be closed or out of a fuel.
  • We don't guarantee that the prices, station details or savings shown are accurate or complete, or that you'll save any money. We aren't responsible for the prices retailers charge.
