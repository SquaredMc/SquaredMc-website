Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026  ·  Applies to the Refuelr iOS app and this website.

The short version

  • There's no account. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Refuelr uses your location only while the app is open, rounded to about 100 metres, to find nearby stations. We don't save it to a database or build a history of where you've been.
  • Your fuel type, car size and other settings stay on your phone.
  • Refuelr is free because it shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google collects some information to show and measure ads, and you choose whether it can personalise them.

1. Who we are

Refuelr is an iOS app that shows fuel prices at UK petrol stations. It's built by SQUAREDMC LTD ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales with company number 16175464, whose registered office is at 17 Poole Road, Epsom, KT19 9SQ.

This policy explains what information the Refuelr app and this website use, why, and your rights. We're the controller of the personal data described here. If you have any questions, email us at hello@squaredmc.com.

2. Location data

If you allow it, Refuelr uses your device's location only while the app is open (iOS calls this "While Using the App"). It never asks for background or "Always" access.

  • What we receive. To find stations near you, the app rounds your position to three decimal places, roughly 100 metres, and sends it to our server along with your search distance. If you search by postcode or town instead, the app sends the rounded position of that place.
  • What we do with it. Our server uses it to look up nearby stations and prices and sends them back. We don't save your location to our database, link it to you or your device, or build a history of where you've been.
  • Server logs. Like most online services, our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps short-term request logs, which include the rounded location. These are deleted automatically after [N] days.
  • Apple. To show a place name (such as "CR0 · Croydon") and to let you search by postcode or town, the app uses Apple's location services, so Apple receives your location or the text you search for. The map also shows your position using Apple Maps. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

If you don't allow location access, you can search by postcode or town instead. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Refuelr.

3. Information you enter

Your fuel type, car size or tank size, search distance, list order, and whether prices show in pounds or pence are stored only on your device, so Refuelr remembers them next time. They aren't sent to us. (Your search distance is sent with each request, as described in section 2, but isn't stored.) Deleting the app deletes them.

4. Fuel price data

Station details and prices come from the UK Government's Fuel Finder scheme, where fuel retailers report their own prices. We copy this data to our server every few hours. It's about fuel stations, not about you.

5. Maps and directions

The map in Refuelr is provided by Apple Maps. When you tap Route here, Refuelr opens the Apple Maps app with the station as your destination. From then on, Apple's privacy policy applies.

6. Advertising

Refuelr is free because it shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, such as your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, device and app information, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. If you allow tracking, it also receives your device's advertising identifier.

  • Tracking permission. When you first open Refuelr, iOS asks whether the app can track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier isn't shared and the ads you see are less personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Your consent choices. In the UK, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change them at any time in Refuelr under Settings → About → Ad preferences.
  • What we don't share. We don't pass Google your precise location, your settings, or anything else you enter in the app.
  • Ads in Refuelr are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Usage, analytics and crash data

Refuelr doesn't include any analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, which don't identify you. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects its own diagnostic and performance data, as described in section 6. If we add analytics in future, we'll update this policy before that collection starts.

8. What we don't collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or phone number. Refuelr doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or calendar, and it doesn't send notifications.

The only exception is if you email us: we'll have your email address and whatever you include, and we'll use them only to reply to you.

10. Legal basis and your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests to use your rounded location to find nearby stations when you open the app, to keep short-term server logs so the service stays secure and reliable, and to show non-personalised ads that keep Refuelr free.
  • Consent for personalised advertising, given through Google's consent form and Apple's tracking prompt. You can withdraw it at any time, as described in section 6.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold information that identifies you (there's no account, and your location isn't stored against you), there's usually nothing we can find to give you or delete. Your settings live only on your device, and deleting the app removes them. For information Google holds for advertising, use Ad preferences in the app or contact Google.

To make a request, email hello@squaredmc.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Children

Refuelr isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes.

13. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address to keep the site secure and running. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit.
