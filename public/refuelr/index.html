FAQ

Common questions.

Can't see yours? Visit Support.

Is Refuelr free?

Yes, Refuelr is free and shows one small banner ad. No account or subscription.

Where do the prices come from?

Prices come from the UK government's Fuel Finder scheme, which requires forecourts to publish their prices. Refuelr refreshes them every few hours.

How up to date are the prices?

Refuelr refreshes prices every few hours and shows the latest price each station has reported. A forecourt can change its price between refreshes, so the price at the pump is the one that counts.

Which fuels does Refuelr cover?

Unleaded E10, Super E5 and Diesel.

Does it work everywhere in the UK?

Refuelr shows forecourts across the UK that report their prices to Fuel Finder. Search within 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 miles of where you are, or of any postcode or town.

Do you track my location?

No. If you allow it, Refuelr uses your location to find stations near you. It isn't stored or used to build a history of where you've been, and you can search by postcode or town instead. The Privacy Policy explains what the app's ad provider collects.

Do I need an account?

No. Your fuel, tank size and other settings stay on your phone.

A price looks wrong. What should I do?

Prices are reported by the retailers and can change before the next refresh. If one still looks wrong, email us with the station and the price you saw, and we'll look into it.

How is the saving worked out?

Refuelr takes the difference between a station's price and the local average for your fuel, then multiplies it by your tank size. 6p a litre less on a 50-litre tank is about £3.00.

Does Refuelr show EV charging?

No. Refuelr covers Unleaded E10, Super E5 and Diesel.

Is Refuelr on Android?

Not at the moment. Refuelr is for iPhone.

How do I get support?

Visit the Support page or email hello@squaredmc.com.
