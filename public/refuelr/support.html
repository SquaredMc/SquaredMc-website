Reporting a wrong price

If a price in Refuelr doesn't match the pump, email us with:

  • the station name or brand, and the town or postcode;
  • the fuel (unleaded E10, super unleaded E5 or diesel);
  • the price you saw at the pump, and the price Refuelr showed; and
  • the date and time you saw it.

A photo of the price sign helps, but only take one when you've parked. Prices are reported by the retailers themselves through the government's Fuel Finder scheme, and Refuelr refreshes them every few hours, so a price can be out of date. Always check the pump before you fill up.

Quick fixes

  • No stations, or the wrong area? Check that location access is on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Refuelr → While Using the App, or search a postcode or town at the top of the Prices or Map tab.
  • Wrong fuel or car size? Change them in the Settings tab in Refuelr. Your car size is used to estimate the saving per tank.
  • Ad choices. Review or change them in Refuelr under Settings → About → Ad preferences, and change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Few stations nearby? Try a larger search distance with the Filter button. In rural areas Refuelr searches further afield automatically.
